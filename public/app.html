EduFocus Mobil Ilovasi

Dars vaqtida diqqatni jamlash va testlarni topshirish uchun qulay rasmiy ilova.

🔑 Darsga Kirish

🤖 Android (Chrome) uchun o'rnatish:

1 Chrome brauzerining yuqori o'ng burchagidagi ⋮ (3 nuqta) menyusini bosing.
2 Menyudan "Ilovani o'rnatish" (yoki "Bosh ekranga qo'shish") tugmasini bosing.
3 Telefonda xuddi ilovadek paydo bo'ladi va brauzersiz ochiladi!

🍎 iPhone (Safari) uchun o'rnatish:

1 Safari brauzerining pastki qismidagi ⎋ (Ulashish / Share) belgisini bosing.
2 Pastga siljitib, "Bosh ekranga qo'shish" (Add to Home Screen) tugmasini bosing.
3 Bosh ekranda to'liq ekranli ilova sifatida ochiladi!
